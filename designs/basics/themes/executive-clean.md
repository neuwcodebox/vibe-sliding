# Executive Clean

## Intended Use

Use this guide for management summaries, decision memos, project status updates, strategy reviews, and proposal decks where clarity and prioritization matter more than visual drama.

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere

- Mood: clear, controlled, trustworthy
- Energy level: medium-low
- Visual density: moderate, optimized for scanning
- Formality: high

## Color Palette & Roles

| Role | Color | Usage |
|---|---:|---|
| Background | #F8FAFC | Main slide background |
| Surface | #FFFFFF | Cards, tables, summary panels |
| Border | #CBD5E1 | Dividers and table rules |
| Primary Text | #0F172A | Titles and key statements |
| Secondary Text | #475569 | Body text |
| Muted Text | #94A3B8 | Captions and metadata |
| Accent | #0F766E | Decisions, highlights, selected values |
| Risk | #B45309 | Risks and constraints |

## Typography Rules

- Use Inter and Noto Sans KR.
- Titles: 64-92px, semibold.
- Section headings: 34-48px, semibold.
- Body: 24-32px, regular.
- Captions: 18-22px.
- Use monospace only for IDs, financial figures, or command snippets.

## Layout Principles

- Use strong top headers and clean horizontal divisions.
- Prefer summary blocks, decision tables, timelines, and 2x2 grids.
- Keep margins between 80px and 120px.
- Use no more than three hierarchy levels on a slide.
- Keep charts large with direct labels.

## Visual Elements

- Cards: white surface, 1px slate border, no nested cards.
- Panels: use for executive summaries or recommendations.
- Lines: slate dividers, 1-2px.
- Icons: minimal line icons, teal or slate.
- Diagrams: business process style, few nodes.
- Tables: readable row height, strong column headers.
- Code blocks: avoid unless necessary.
- Charts: clear labels, restrained palette, no chart clutter.

## Motion

- Avoid motion for final presentation decks.
- If used, apply a single fade-in to the slide body.

## Do

- Lead with the answer or recommendation.
- Use direct labels such as "Decision", "Risk", and "Next step".
- Keep numbers large and easy to compare.
- Use Korean and English consistently in one font stack.

## Don't

- Do not use playful illustration.
- Do not bury the recommendation in dense bullets.
- Do not use more than two accent colors.
- Do not rely on color alone for risk/status.

## Responsive Behavior

- Treat slides as a fixed 16:9 canvas.
- The viewer scales the canvas to the viewport.
- Do not design slides as vertically scrolling webpages.
