# Startup Pitch

## Intended Use

Use this guide for product vision decks, fundraising narratives, demo introductions, launch presentations, and energetic internal storytelling.

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere

- Mood: bold, optimistic, product-led
- Energy level: high
- Visual density: medium
- Formality: polished but expressive

## Color Palette & Roles

| Role | Color | Usage |
|---|---:|---|
| Background | #101828 | Main dark hero background |
| Surface | #FFFFFF | High-contrast product cards |
| Surface Dark | #1D2939 | Dark panels and callouts |
| Border | #D0D5DD | Light card outlines |
| Primary Text | #FFFFFF | Dark hero headings |
| Primary Text Light | #101828 | Light section headings |
| Secondary Text | #D0D5DD | Dark slide body text |
| Accent | #FF6B4A | Energy, CTAs, key numbers |
| Accent 2 | #12B76A | Proof, growth, positive movement |

## Typography Rules

- Use Inter and Noto Sans KR.
- Hero titles: 92-128px, bold or semibold.
- Section titles: 60-86px.
- Body: 28-36px.
- Captions: 18-24px.
- Monospace only for metrics or demo commands.

## Layout Principles

- Use strong hero moments with clear product or customer value.
- Keep each slide anchored by one memorable claim.
- Combine large metric blocks with short proof points.
- Prefer diagonal energy through composition, not through text rotation.
- Keep enough whitespace around product screenshots or mockups.

## Visual Elements

- Cards: white or dark high-contrast cards, 6-8px radius.
- Panels: use for traction, demo steps, or customer pain.
- Lines: bold accent dividers or simple paths.
- Icons: friendly lucide icons, 36-56px.
- Diagrams: simple before/after or flywheel structures.
- Tables: avoid unless used for clear competitor comparison.
- Code blocks: rare, only for developer product demos.
- Charts: emphasize growth trajectory and milestones.

## Motion

- Short entrance motion is allowed.
- Avoid bouncing, spinning, or distracting loops.
- Motion should support reveal order.

## Do

- Lead with a clear product promise.
- Use one strong accent color per slide.
- Make metrics large and direct.
- Use visuals that show product state, workflow, or result.

## Don't

- Do not make text-heavy investor memos.
- Do not use generic stock-like imagery.
- Do not overuse gradients.
- Do not put the main promise inside a small card.

## Responsive Behavior

- Treat slides as a fixed 16:9 canvas.
- The viewer scales the canvas to the viewport.
- Do not design slides as vertically scrolling webpages.
